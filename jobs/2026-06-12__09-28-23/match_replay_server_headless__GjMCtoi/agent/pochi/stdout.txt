The headless backend supplies a real `HeadlessApplication` that drives the `ApplicationListener` lifecycle on a dedicated thread, plus mock implementations of `Graphics`, `Audio`, and `Input` so the rest of libGDX (`Gdx.files`, math, JSON, `Array`, etc.) still works.
