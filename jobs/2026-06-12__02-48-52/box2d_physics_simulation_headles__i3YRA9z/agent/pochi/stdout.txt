## How it works

1. **`Launcher.main()`** parses `--scenario=<path>`, calls `ScenarioParser.parse()`, then creates a `HeadlessApplicationConfiguration` with `updatesPerSecond=0` (run as fast as possible) and boots `new HeadlessApplication(simulator, config)`. A `CountDownLatch(1)` blocks the main thread until `dispose()` fires.

2. **`PhysicsSimulator`** (an `ApplicationAdapter`):
   - `create()` — calls `Box2D.init()`, creates the `World` with scripted gravity, creates the single dynamic body (circular fixture, radius 0.5 m, density 1.0, zero friction/restitution/damping), optionally overrides mass via `setMassData()`, then builds a `List<List<float[]>>` impulse map indexed by step.
   - `render()` — on each call applies any impulses for `currentStep`, calls `world.step(1/60, 6, 2)`, increments the counter. When all steps are exhausted a `done` flag prevents re-entry, prints `Final position: (%.4f, %.4f)`, and calls `Gdx.app.exit()`.
   - `dispose()` — disposes the world and counts down the latch.
