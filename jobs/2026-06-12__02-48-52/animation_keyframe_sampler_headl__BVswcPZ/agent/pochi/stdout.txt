## Key design decisions

### `Launcher.java`
* Validates that two CLI arguments are present (config path, output path).
* Creates a `HeadlessApplicationConfiguration` with `updatesPerSecond = 0` (non-blocking render loop) and hands it to `HeadlessApplication`.

### `AnimationSampler.java`
* Runs entirely inside `create()` so it executes once on startup, then calls `Gdx.app.exit()`.
* Config parsing:
  * Lines starting with `#` or blank lines are skipped.
  * `frameDuration`, `playMode`, and `keyFrames` are read exactly once.
  * `sample` lines are collected in order (preserving the original string token verbatim).
* Builds `Animation<Integer>` with a `com.badlogic.gdx.utils.Array<Integer>` and the parsed `PlayMode`.
* For each sample, calls `animation.getKeyFrame(stateTime)` (play-mode-aware overload) and `animation.isAnimationFinished(stateTime)`.
* Writes output via `FileOutputStream(path, false)` (overwrite, not append) with UTF-8 encoding.

### `build.gradle`
* Depends on `gdx`, `gdx-backend-headless`, and `gdx-platform:natives-desktop` at 1.14.2 (all pre-cached).
* `application { mainClass = 'com.example.Launcher' }` exposes the `run` task.
* `compileJava.options.encoding = 'UTF-8'` suppresses compiler warnings.
