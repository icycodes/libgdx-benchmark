The root node's parent is the identity transform (world position `(0,0)`, world rotation `0`, world scale `(1,1)`). 1. Scale the local offset by the parent's world scale: `lx = N.x * P.worldScaleX`, `ly = N.y * P.worldScaleY`. 2. Compose scale (component-wise): `N.worldScaleX = P.worldScaleX * N.scaleX`, `N.worldScaleY = P.worldScaleY * N.scaleY`.
